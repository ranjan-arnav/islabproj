Step 1 of 2

Join an encrypted room

Everyone who should read the chat must enter the exact same room code. Share it in person, not in this chat.

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 derives the AES key locally. The code is never sent over Bluetooth.
Step 2 of 2

Connect a paired laptop

  1. Pair both laptops in Windows/macOS/Linux Bluetooth settings.
  2. Keep this app running on both.
  3. Enter the other laptop's Bluetooth MAC address below.
Only one side needs to connect. The other side accepts the link automatically on channel 12.
Security design

Three layers, one packet

01AES-256-GCMEncrypts the message and detects ciphertext tampering.
02RSA-OAEP + RSA-PSSPrivate messages are sealed to one recipient and every message is signed.
03Opaque multi-hop relayIntermediate laptops forward private ciphertext without displaying or decrypting it.

Room broadcasts are readable by room members. Choose a named recipient in the composer for recipient-only end-to-end encryption.
